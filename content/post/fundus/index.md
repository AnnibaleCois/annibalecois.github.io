We concluded the first phase of the pilot project investigating the use of artifical intelligence for diabetic retinopathy screening and vascular risk stratification. 
The study involved 200 diabetic patients in a endocroinologist practice in the Western Cape Province and produce valuable info for future development of low-cost screening procedures for diabetes 
complications.

See more detail in the [project page](/project/retinopathy/), or watch the [videosummary](https://youtu.be/5ryF8YqDxAU) of the main results on youtube.
